## Control arm (`ctl_1g`): redefined around `srlatch_buf`, still fails as documented

`make_bench.py`'s `SIBLING_POLARITY` arm was retargeted with this issue: it now
instantiates the committed `srlatch_buf` (7-pin order `S R Q QB QN VDD VSS`)
with the same `nand2x_lv` NAND tap on the `QN` storage nodes as `pfd`, and
differs from `pfd` in exactly one way — the sibling's reset polarity (two
inverters after the NAND, reset = NAND output) instead of the redrawn one
(single inverter, reset = NAND complement). `srlatch_fast` is gone from
`design/netlist/pfd.spice` (no consumer remains); the choice is recorded here
and in `make_bench.py`'s comment. Behaviour with the new latch matches the arm's
documented failure: every graded offset shows `(wUP−wDN)/offset ≈ 0` (dead
zone) and the reset-pulse width reads 0..0 ps — C1 and C2 FAIL at all three
corners, i.e. the polarity, not the latch, is what the sibling arrangement gets
wrong on these lv cells (the 0008/0011 argument, now isolated on the committed
cell). The control's FAIL does not affect the gate verdict (`grade.py` grades
only `pfd_*`).

## EE-review findings carried from PR #152 (operator ruling)

1. **4 fF bench load derived, not estimated.** `LOAD_F`'s real referent is the
   adopted CP input stage: `design/netlist/cp.spice`'s `XIUP`/`XIDN` `inv_lv`
   inputs (P 1 µm + N 0.5 µm gates) plus the switch gates (`XLEGP XXSWD`
   P 3 µm on UP, `XLEGN XXSWO` N 1 µm on DN), all L = 0.13 µm. Method: local
   ngspice 47 with the same OSDI models, CP biased through `cp_bias`,
   quasi-static 1 µs ramps per corner, `C_eff = |∫I dt|/VDD` per lane per
   direction (deck + logs retained in `load-derivation/`). Result:
   **UP 4.52/4.37/4.66 fF, DN 4.71/4.45/4.97 fF at tt/ss/ff** (rise/fall agree
   within 0.3 %) — the 4 fF model is 9..24 % light. Headroom, why the model is
   kept: the miss loads only the `inv4x` driver output, whose rise and fall
   both carry it, so the C2 width (fall − rise difference through the driver)
   moves by ≲ 2 ps for the full +1 fF (driver edge pair at ss: 50.6/44.0 ps at
   4 fF); the ss margin is 42.2 ps; and the common-mode part is exactly what
   C1's ratio band grades. Changing `LOAD_F` would also break the byte-stable
   0006 stimulus and the probe reconciliation above for a sub-ps-class effect,
   so the re-run-at-derived-load branch of the finding was not taken; a future
   re-run at 5 fF is the first thing to try if ss margin ever shrinks.
2. **Like-for-like floor comparison.** The 0015 "≥ 24.1 ps floor" row compared
   the candidate's UP-referenced `path` (30.8 ps) against a floor derived from
   the committed design's UP timing — unlike references. Like against like,
   against the set-pulse end (the internal reference both designs share): the
   committed design's storage node flipped 24.1 ps before `set_up` fell; the
   rewired design's `up_qn` flips 29.8 ps before it (probe above, both at
   ss/1.080 V/125 °C) — the internal ordering C1 needs is preserved with
   +5.7 ps more margin, and the reset still rises 127.4 ps after the set pulse
   ends (0015's unwired probe: 127.5 ps). The UP-referenced identities of 0015's budget transfer to the internal
   nodes for exactly this reason; the gate, not these identities, is the
   arbiter.
3. **Common-mode at nonzero offset.** Established by this run: C1 ratios
   1.050..1.161 at ss/1 GHz (1.023..1.180 across all corners and both
   frequencies) at every graded offset down to ±10 ps — no dead zone, no
   offset-dependent skew from the driver.
4. **Not recoverable.** The stored copy of the EE review (PR #152 comment
   7033692275) is truncated after finding 2 ("[truncated: last 2141 characters
   omitted]" is in the stored body itself; no untruncated copy exists on the
   forge or in the repo at head `353efd541`). Findings 3–4 were carried as:
   finding 3 recovered from the review's own per-row table ("Not demonstrated
   at nonzero offset … See finding 3 below", addressed above); finding 4 could
   not be recovered and is flagged here for the operator. This record is the
   place to re-state finding 4 verbatim if a copy surfaces.
